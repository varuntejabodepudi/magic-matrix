# MagicMatrix Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

MagicMatrix is a magic-square puzzle game developed by **Bodepudi Varun Teja**.

This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the MagicMatrix Android application. The repository is currently a documentation-only public repository; the precise data flows, third-party SDK configuration, and release build behaviour must be verified against the actual app before publication of a production privacy declaration.

## 1. Information and gameplay data

The game may create or use gameplay information such as puzzle progress, selected settings, completion records, scores, and achievements to provide game features.

The exact storage location, retention period, and synchronization behaviour depend on the implementation and enabled integrations in the particular app release. Do not assume that data is stored only on-device or that it is never transmitted until the shipped build and its SDKs have been audited.

## 2. Accounts

An account is not required to understand or play the core MagicMatrix puzzle concept. Features supplied by optional third-party platforms, if enabled in a release, may have their own account requirements and privacy terms.

## 3. Permissions and device access

The permissions requested by MagicMatrix depend on the Android release. The app should request only permissions needed for features that are actually present. Consult the permission disclosures on the Google Play listing and Android system prompts for the installed version.

## 4. Third-party services

A release may include optional platform integrations or software-development kits. Where such services are enabled, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. The final production policy should identify each active provider and describe the data it receives.

## 5. Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not intend to sell personal information. Any information sharing necessary for a user-enabled third-party feature must be documented in the production policy before that feature is released.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data retention and deletion depend on the storage mechanisms used by the installed version. Removing the app may delete locally stored app data, but may not delete data retained by a third-party service. Follow that service's procedures for requests relating to its records.

## 7. Children's privacy

MagicMatrix is a puzzle game. The developer does not knowingly seek personal information from children through this repository. The app's age rating, target audience, advertising configuration, and any child-directed features must be reviewed before distribution.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, integrations, or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published.
